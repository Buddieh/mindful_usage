Heating pipes that run through unheated spaces, such as a crawl space, attic or garage, give off heat before the water reaches your radiators. Pipe insulation from a DIY shop is cheap and quick to fit.

The circulation pump that moves water through the radiators can use a lot of electricity. An old pump that runs at one speed all season can use up to about 450 kWh a year. If yours has speed settings, try the lowest one: if every radiator still gets warm, you save up to 120 kWh a season.

When the pump needs replacing, choose a high-efficiency pump that adjusts itself and switches off when there is no demand for heat.
