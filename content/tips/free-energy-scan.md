Eligible: households below an income limit (in 2026: €29,830 for a single person; €44,740 for a couple or a single person with one dependent; plus €4,420 per extra dependent), all social tenants regardless of income, and Fluvius customers with a prepaid meter. Income is checked automatically when you apply. Apply online via mijnenergiescan.vlaanderen.be, or ask your OCMW, CAW or Energiehuis to apply for you.
