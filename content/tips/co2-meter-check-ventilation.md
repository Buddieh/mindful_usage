A CO2 meter does not detect harmful substances, but it shows whether a room gets enough fresh air. Choose one with an NDIR sensor that measures real CO2, put it at about 1.5 m high away from windows, vents and people, and check that it reads about 400 to 500 ppm outdoors.

There is no official limit for homes. Flemish guidance for care facilities uses 900 ppm as the target and 1,200 ppm as the upper limit for acceptable indoor air, which is a reasonable guide at home too. {nocite}

When the reading is high, ventilate more: turn up the ventilation system, open windows, or air across the home. In cold weather the meter also helps you avoid airing longer than needed.
