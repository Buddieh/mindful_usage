The Energiehuis can help you understand your bill, compare contracts and decide what is worth asking your landlord for. Find yours via mijnenergiehuis.be.

Some services, such as home visits by a renovation coach, are only for owners. As a tenant you can ask the Energiehuis about a subsidised energy scan if your income is low enough.
