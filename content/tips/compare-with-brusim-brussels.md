Have your latest annual settlement bill (facture de régularisation) for electricity or gas at hand, then enter your details in BruSim. You get a list of suppliers with their prices, split into fixed-price and variable-price offers.

BruSim itself warns that its estimates for variable and dynamic contracts do not take the recent rise in wholesale prices into account, and that some suppliers are changing the terms of their fixed-price contracts. Before you switch, read the supplier's price sheet and the specific conditions of the offer you pick.
