High-efficiency glass has a thin metal coating and a gas filling such as argon. It insulates two to three times better than ordinary double glazing and four to five times better than single glazing. Its U-value is about 1.1 W/m²K, against 2.9 for old double glazing and 5.8 for single glazing; the lower the number, the better.

To see what you have, look for a U-value or product name on the spacer bar between the panes, or hold a flame near the glass: a reflection with a different colour points to a coating. That test is not always reliable.

New windows make a home more airtight. Plan air supply in the dry rooms at the same time, with grilles in the new windows or a ventilation system, to avoid condensation and mould.
