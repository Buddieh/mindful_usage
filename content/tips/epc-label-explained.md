Since 2019 the EPC (energy performance certificate) shows a label from F (red) to A+ (dark green) and an energy score in kWh per m² per year. The lower the score, the better.

The score is calculated, not measured: it assumes the whole home is heated to the same temperature with standard ventilation, so your actual use is often lower. It is useful for comparing homes and for seeing what a renovation would improve.

Only a recognised energy expert (type A) can draw one up, after a visit. It is valid for up to 10 years and is required to sell or rent out a home. Older EPCs from before 2019 show a score but no label. You can see your current EPC in the Woningpas, but that copy is not signed.
