Suppliers may not charge you an exit fee when you switch. Watch out for the yearly fixed fee: if your old supplier charges it per contract year, start the new contract the day after the old one ends. Have your yearly consumption (on your annual bill or in Mijn Fluvius) at hand and run it through the V-test. Creating a personal profile lets it use your actual meter data.
