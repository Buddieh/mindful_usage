Mould needs moisture. Let fresh air in and moist air out every day. Cook with a lid on the pan, use the extractor hood, and dry laundry outside or in a dryer. Keep rooms above 15 °C and avoid big temperature differences and cold walls, so condensation doesn't form. In winter, indoor humidity between 40 and 60% is a good range.

Some damp comes from the building itself: water rising from the ground into the wall, leaks in a gutter or pipe, rain seeping in, or cold spots (cold bridges) where moisture collects. Fix these as soon as you can.

You can remove mould yourself with gloves and a mask, but avoid bleach. Don't do it yourself if you have breathing problems or a mould allergy. As long as the material stays damp, the mould will come back, so if it keeps returning, call in a professional.
