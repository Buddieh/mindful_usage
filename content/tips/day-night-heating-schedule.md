Program the thermostat to drop to 16 °C (or lower) at night and when nobody is home, and to warm up shortly before you get up or come home.

With underfloor heating, don't drop below about 18 °C for normal absences, because it reacts slowly. Avoid letting poorly ventilated rooms fall below 10 °C, which increases the risk of condensation and mould.
