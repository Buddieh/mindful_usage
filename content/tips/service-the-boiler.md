Unless your lease says otherwise, the tenant arranges periodic maintenance of the central heating boiler; the landlord handles the first inspection, major repairs and replacement. Keep the certificate the technician gives you and pass a copy to your landlord.

Frequency depends on fuel and power: oil boilers yearly; gas boilers of 20–100 kW every two years. Small gas boilers under 20 kW may not be legally required to be serviced, but it is still recommended.
