Owners and co-owners can log in at woningpas.vlaanderen.be with eID or itsme. You find the EPC, EPB declarations, permits, insulation and installation details, inspection and asbestos certificates, and a step-by-step renovation plan with cost indications and advice on sustainable heating.

You can share it with a contractor or renovation coach through a code or link that expires. The digital EPC is not signed, so you still need the signed EPC certificate to sell or rent out the home.
