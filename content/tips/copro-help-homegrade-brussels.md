Homegrade, the Brussels advice centre for housing, supports small co-ownerships that are run without a professional syndic. Its services are free and non-commercial.

Homegrade can explain how the co-ownership is organised and how decisions are taken, carry out a pre-diagnosis of the common parts so you know which works to tackle first, help you analyse and compare quotes for joint works, and help prepare the general meeting. It does not give legal advice and does not act as syndic.

If your building is managed by a professional syndic, the page points to the Facilitateur Bâtiment Durable instead.
