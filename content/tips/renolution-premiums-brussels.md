Bruxelles Environnement states that the RENOLUTION premiums are suspended for now, and that the Brussels government has not yet decided on any new form of financial support for renovation. The official sites say they will publish the news as soon as there is a decision, so renolution.brussels and the Bruxelles Environnement premium page are the places to check.

The RENOLUTION premium page now only concerns works with a final invoice from 2024, and new applications for those are no longer possible; if you already applied, you can follow your file on IRISbox. Homegrade also says that renovation support for 2026 is not yet known. For an up-to-date view of what support exists for your works, contact Homegrade, which the Bruxelles Environnement page names as the contact for individuals.
