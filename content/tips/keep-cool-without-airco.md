A room overheats when it stays above 25 °C for more than an hour in summer. Well-insulated, airtight homes heat up faster, because the heat that comes in stays in. Insulation still helps in summer, but it needs shading and good airing to go with it.

Most of the heat comes in as sunlight through windows. Screens or roller shutters on the outside work best, especially on windows facing south or west. Blinds or curtains inside help far less, because the heat is already through the glass.

Open windows when it is cooler outside than inside, usually at night, and keep them closed while it is hotter outside. Airco can push up your electricity bill sharply, so try shading and night air first.
