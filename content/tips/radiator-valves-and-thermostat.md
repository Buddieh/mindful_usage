Thermostatic valves keep each room at its own temperature, so bedrooms and hallways stay cooler while the living room is warm. Suggested settings are about 20 °C (valve setting 3) in the living room, kitchen and study, and 16 to 18 °C (setting 2 to 2.5) in bedrooms. In the room with the thermostat, open the valve fully.

Choose a room thermostat with at least two programmes, one for the day and one for the night, and place it in the living room. A smart thermostat that heats according to when you are home suits irregular schedules or anyone who finds programming difficult.
