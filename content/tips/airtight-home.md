Warm air escapes through cracks, gaps and especially the joints where building parts meet, such as between a window frame and the wall, or a roof and a wall. That causes draughts, heat loss, condensation and mould, even in a well-insulated house. A renovation, when walls and roofs are open, is the moment to make those joints airtight.

Airtightness can be measured, by a tester recognised by SKH or BCCA. A measurement carried out under the official quality framework can be used in the EPB calculation, where good airtightness lowers the E-level (E-peil).

An airtight house needs controlled ventilation to keep the air healthy and avoid damp. Plan the two together.
