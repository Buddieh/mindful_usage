Every connected thermostat, plug or camera is a small computer on your network. Before you buy, check whether the maker updates its products regularly, and avoid very cheap devices without a recognised brand. Change the default password as soon as you install a device, and don't reuse the same password on several devices.

Install updates as soon as they appear, or turn on automatic updates. Secure your Wi-Fi with a strong password, give visitors a separate guest network, and keep your modem up to date. Switch off features you don't use, and now and then check which devices are connected and remove any you don't recognise.

Your digital meter's data is yours. The law sets who may use it, and anyone who wants it for another purpose needs your explicit consent. You decide whether the meter's user port is open and which device reads it, and any device plugged into an open port can read everything it sends. Be wary of emails, texts or WhatsApp messages in Fluvius' name asking for money or details.
