The Flemish government's advice is simple: don't wait until your boiler fails to think about alternatives to oil or gas. A boiler that breaks down has to be replaced quickly, which leaves no time to insulate first or compare options.

Follow the usual order: insulate, move to low-temperature heating, then choose a heat pump or heat network. If the house isn't well insulated yet, a hybrid heat pump, which works with a gas boiler, can bridge the gap. A condensing gas boiler is still possible where there is a gas connection. The Warmtepompklaar tool and an installer's assessment help you decide.

Rules are moving away from fossil heating. Since 1 July 2025, replacing a gas boiler carries 21% VAT instead of 6%, while heat pumps in renovations stay at 6%. Oil boilers are restricted since 2022. Check the official page for the current rules.
