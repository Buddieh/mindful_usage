Since 1 October 2026, the Walloon renovation loans (Rénopack and Rénoprêt) start with an audit logement (the Walloon home audit). The report must have been made or updated in the year before you apply, and the works you finance with the loan have to appear in it. Its cost can be included in the loan. For an apartment building there is a specific audit for the whole building.

Only an auditor accredited by the Walloon administration may carry it out; accredited auditors have passed a training and an exam and are on an official list. The auditor visits the home, records its state, and checks basic safety, health and habitability as well as energy use. The report gives the home's PEB label and proposes bundles of works ranked by priority, with the energy gain of each, so you know what to tackle first. The auditor explains the report to you in person.

The scheme's procedures are still being finalised, but the SWCS advises you can already have the audit done and collect quotes. A Guichet Énergie can explain the audit and what follows before you book one.

This does not apply in the nine German-speaking municipalities, where the German-speaking Community runs its own support; see ostbelgienlive.be.
