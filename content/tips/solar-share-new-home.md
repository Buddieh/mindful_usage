The EPB rules ask every new home to produce part of its energy itself. For permits applied for from 2025, that means at least 15 kWh per m² a year from solar energy. Only the home's own solar panels or solar water heater count. A heat pump can still heat the home, but it no longer counts towards this share.

Instead of solar on the home, there is one alternative: full participation in a renewable energy project, set 10 kWh per m² higher than the normal requirement. If the home meets neither, its E-level limit becomes stricter: E26 instead of E30.
