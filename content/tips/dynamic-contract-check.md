With a dynamic contract the price changes every quarter hour with the market. It can pay off if you can run heavy appliances (an electric car, a heat pump, a washing machine on a timer) when prices are low. If most of your use falls in the evening, when prices tend to be high, a fixed or variable contract is usually safer. {nocite} The V-test includes dynamic offers; with a digital meter and more than a year of data you can import your quarter-hour usage for a realistic estimate.
