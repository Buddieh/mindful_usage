EPB (energy performance and indoor climate) rules set the limits a new home in Flanders must meet. The limits depend on the year you apply for the permit. For applications from 2025, a new home may reach at most E30 for its overall energy performance (the E-level) and at most S28 for the insulation of its shell (the S-level). S29 to S31 is also allowed if the home reaches E20 or better.

Each part of the shell has its own limit too. Roofs, walls and floors may have a U-value of at most 0.24 W/m²K, and windows at most 1.5, with glass of at most 1.1.

The home also needs a ventilation system, and its overheating indicator must stay below 6,500 Kh. Since 2025 a new home can't get a gas connection. The heating must work at a low temperature (a design flow temperature of at most 45 °C), and central heating with water must reach an installation efficiency of at least 130%. Part of the energy must come from the home's own solar panels or solar water heater.

EPB sets no limit on airtightness, but a measurement done under the official quality framework counts in the calculation. According to vlaanderen.be, you need such a blower-door test to reach a low enough S-level. The EPB-pedia table for your application year has the full list.
