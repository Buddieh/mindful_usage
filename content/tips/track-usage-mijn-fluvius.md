Log in at mijn.fluvius.be with your eID or itsme to see consumption graphs, usually up to yesterday. Look at your night-time baseline: if usage stays high while everyone sleeps, something (an old fridge, a dehumidifier, an electric boiler) is drawing power constantly.

For live data, a dongle in the meter's user port (gebruikerspoort) and a compatible app show consumption second by second.
