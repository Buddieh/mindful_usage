Cooking on gas releases combustion gases as well as moisture, and that is unhealthy. Measurements in Flemish homes found that a gas stove can raise the level of nitrogen dioxide (NO2) in the living room.

Always use the cooker hood when you cook, preferably one that vents to the outside, and leave it running for a while after cooking. If the hood works poorly or doesn't vent outside, open a window during and after cooking.

When you replace your stove, the Flemish health department advises choosing an electric one.
